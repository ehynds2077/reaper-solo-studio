Solo Studio is a recording desk for a musician working alone. The visual priority is the transport, followed by instrument selection and a take list. Muted slate surfaces (#292E36, #363D47), pale text (#EDF0F2), blue selections (#5994C2), rose recording controls (#BA5763), and amber favorites (#E8B859) distinguish functional states. Helvetica at 13/16/19/28 keeps the native panel readable. Large transport targets sit above compact review controls; notes and recordings are the content. No animation, ornamental cards, or external UI dependencies.

Song timeline is a full-width arrangement editor beneath the shared recording transport. A bar ruler, proportional colored blocks, and a gold playhead communicate the song structure. Edge handles, live drag previews, and a selected-section inspector support direct editing or exact numeric bounds. Shared boundaries move together. The Takes & click view retains review, comping, and click controls. Both views share recording scope and instrument selection. The native panel remains 1200 × 730.
